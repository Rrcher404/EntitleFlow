MAJOR ISSUES REQUIRING RESOLUTION
=====================================================================

ISSUE 1: Road Access - Single Point of Access
The subdivision provides only one road access point (Brookside Lane) to Old Concord
Road. Per Charlotte Zoning Code § 27-3.3.3 and NCGS § 160D-908, subdivisions with
8+ lots must provide secondary emergency access. Alternative: (a) Provide secondary
road connection to adjacent property (with recorded cross-easement), or (b) Reduce
lot count to 7 lots.

ISSUE 2: Lot 4 Frontage Deficiency
Lot 4 has only 92 feet of frontage on Brookside Lane. Minimum required is 100 feet
per zoning code § 27-4.2.1.1. Reconfigure to provide 100+ feet frontage or reduce
lot count to 7 lots.

ISSUE 3: Stormwater Management Plan - Missing TRC Approval
Subdivision plat requires approval stamp from Charlotte-Mecklenburg Stormwater
Services indicating compliance with NCAC T15A.02D and local standards. Currently
missing. Submit to Stormwater Services with revised grading plan showing detention
basin routing for 25-year storm event.

ISSUE 4: HOA/Common Area - Maintenance Responsibility
Eight lots are served by common road (Brookside Lane) and common stormwater detention
basin. Plat must include: (a) HOA formation documents showing maintenance responsibility,
(b) Recorded plat note requiring homeowner assessment for road/basin maintenance,
(c) Trail covenant if equestrian easement is shown on plat.

=====================================================================
STANDARD COMMENTS REQUIRING RESOLUTION
=====================================================================

5. ROAD SECTION AND CONSTRUCTION STANDARDS
   Brookside Lane cross-section shows 18-foot paved surface (two 9-foot lanes).
   Charlotte minimum is 20 feet for subdivision roads per Charlotte Design Manual
   Section 2.1.1. Revise to 20-foot width or provide truck turning radius analysis
   showing 16-foot width is adequate for emergency vehicles.

6. SIDEWALK REQUIREMENT
   No sidewalk shown along Brookside Lane. Subdivision roads serving 5+ lots must
   have 5-foot sidewalk per CZC § 27-3.3.4. Add sidewalk on one side (east side
   preferred for sun exposure minimization) or provide variance justification.

7. UTILITY EASEMENT WIDTH
   Water and sanitary sewer easements shown as 10 feet width. Standard is 15 feet
   minimum for joint utilities. Revise easement boundaries on revised plat.

8. DETENTION BASIN - DESIGN DETAILS MISSING
   Stormwater detention basin is shown as polygon on plat but lacks cross-section,
   outlet design, maintenance access, and emergency spillway details. Submit detailed
   stormwater design plan per Charlotte-Mecklenburg BMP Manual.

9. TREE PRESERVATION AREA
   Site has 6-acre undisturbed wooded area on south boundary (wetland adjacent).
   Clarify tree preservation on this area: will it be dedicated to city, remain in
   private ownership, or be subject to conservation easement?

10. BOUNDARY SURVEY CERTIFICATION
    Plat shows bearings and distances but is not signed by North Carolina licensed
    surveyor. Plat must bear certification stamp and signature of NC PLS per NCGS
    § 47E-1.

=====================================================================
MINOR COMMENTS - RECOMMENDATIONS
=====================================================================

11. LIGHTING PLAN FOR SUBDIVISION ROADS
    Brookside Lane lacks street lighting design. While not required for subdivision roads,
    provide option for owner to install street lights if desired. Include utility
    coordination for power source.

12. RIPARIAN BUFFER CALCULATION
    Wetland is shown on south boundary. Verify 50-foot riparian buffer from wetland
    edge is maintained per NCGS § 143-214.7 and CZC § 27-3.5. Current buffer appears
    marginal (approximately 48 feet at closest point).

13. SLOPE STABILITY
    Lot 6 has hillside topography (40% slope). Recommend geotechnical assessment for
    hillside development per NC Building Code. Grading plan should be prepared by
    licensed professional engineer.

14. UTILITIES COORDINATION
    Duke Energy and Verizon easements are not shown on plat. Confirm with utilities
    that service can be extended along Brookside Lane or if overhead/underground service
    is required.

15. PHASING PLAN
    If development is phased, submit phasing plan showing timing and sequencing of
    infrastructure. Specify which lots can be developed independently versus clusters.
