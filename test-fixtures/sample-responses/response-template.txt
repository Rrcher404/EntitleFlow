includes spacing calculations and measurement confirmation. A copy of the revised
sheet is attached.

[If comment partially resolved:]
The applicant acknowledges this concern. [Explain proposed alternative or request
variance if needed.] A traffic impact study is attached showing [specific analysis
or justification].

Comment T-2: [Original comment]

Response: Service entrance driveway sight triangle has been cleared of all
landscaping and street furniture. Sight distance now exceeds AASHTO standards
(minimum 250 feet along Riverside Drive, 25 feet perpendicular). Revised Plan Sheet
C-2 includes sight distance diagram with measurements verified by the project civil
engineer. The utility box on Holden Road has been relocated outside the sight
triangle.

--------

Comment T-3: [Original comment]

Response: A dedicated 100-foot left-turn lane has been added to the southbound
Riverside Drive approach with 6-foot standard taper per NCGS § 136-66 specifications.
This improvement was coordinated with NCDOT (see attached NCDOT Concurrence Letter
dated [date]). Left-turn lane design is shown on revised Traffic Plan Sheet T-2.

========================================================================
STORMWATER COMMENTS
========================================================================

Comment S-1: [Original comment]

Response: The applicant engaged [Stormwater Engineering Firm] to recalculate the
bioretention basin sizing per the NC DWI Stormwater BMP Manual (2022 Update). The
basin has been enlarged to 2.4 acres (from 1.8 acres) to accommodate the 25-year
storm event (81.3 inches annual rainfall per NCAC T15A.02D). Detailed calculations
are attached (Appendix A). The revised basin is shown on Stormwater Plan Sheet S-1.

--------

Comment S-2: [Original comment]

Response: The outfall at the northeast corner has been stabilized per NC DWI
specifications. An 8-foot x 15-foot riprap apron with geotextile underdrain has
been designed and is shown on Stormwater Plan Sheet S-3. A vegetated buffer strip
(25-foot minimum) has been established downstream per riparian buffer protection
requirements.

--------

Comment S-3: [Original comment]

Response: The detention basin outlet structure has been redesigned with a multi-stage
outlet orifice system that restricts discharge to pre-development rate (0.08 cfs/acre
for 2-year storm event). Outlet structure details are shown on Sheet S-4 with
structural calculations by [Engineer Name, PE]. The basin will now provide both
quality and quantity control.

========================================================================
ZONING AND PLANNING COMMENTS
========================================================================

Comment Z-1: [Original comment]

Response: The applicant acknowledges the 4-foot encroachment into the 50-foot
riparian buffer. A variance application has been prepared and is being submitted
separately to the Planning Director per NCGS § 160D-701. The variance submission
includes stream restoration design prepared by [Environmental Consultant] as
compensatory mitigation. The restoration plan (0.25 acres, 150 native plants) is
shown on Environmental Plan Sheet ENV-2.

[Alternative language if proceeding with variance pending:]
The applicant will submit variance application within 5 business days. Riparian
restoration remains committed as condition of approval regardless of variance outcome.

--------

Comment Z-2: [Original comment]

Response: The applicant has reduced parking from 450 to 210 spaces per the City's
1.75 spaces/unit standard for mixed-income housing. A parking demand study is
attached (Appendix B) showing that the project will achieve sustainability goals
through on-street parking in the mixed-use corridor and planned Phase 2 transit
connections. Revised Parking Plan is shown on Sheet P-1.

--------

Comment Z-3: [Original comment]

Response: The monument sign has been reduced to 8 feet height to comply with Zoning
Ordinance Section 8.3.4. The sign face area remains within 50 square foot limit.
Revised sign elevation is shown on Architectural Plan Sheet A-5.

========================================================================
FIRE AND LIFE SAFETY COMMENTS
========================================================================

Comment F-1: [Original comment]

Response: A second emergency access drive has been added from Holden Road per NFPA
101 Life Safety Code Section 7.5.1.2. The drive is 22 feet wide paved surface
extending to all building exits. Secondary access provides circulation around the
entire structure and connects to the primary Riverside Drive access for continuous
egress. Emergency Access Plan is shown on Sheet ES-1.

--------

Comment F-2: [Original comment]

Response: Fire hydrant spacing has been revised per NFPA 14 spacing requirements.
Six (6) hydrants are now provided (one per 5,000 sq ft of building area for the
85,000 sq ft structure). Water Department has confirmed capacity for six hydrants
on the proposed 8-inch main extension. Hydrant locations are shown on Site Plan
Sheet SP-2 and coordinated with Water Department letter (attached).

========================================================================
LANDSCAPE AND ENVIRONMENTAL COMMENTS
========================================================================

Comment L-1: [Original comment]

Response: The existing tree cluster (8 oaks, 65 DBH average) in the northeast corner
has been incorporated into the site design and will be preserved. A Tree Preservation
Plan prepared by [ISA Certified Arborist Name] is attached with protective fencing
during construction and post-construction maintenance specifications. The trees will
remain in place as a native canopy feature and will be integrated into the riparian
restoration area (Comment ENV-4).

--------

Comment ENV-4: [Original comment]

Response: Riparian buffer restoration design is being prepared by [Environmental
Consultant] and will be submitted with the variance application (Comment Z-1). The
0.25-acre restoration area will include:
  - 50 native understory trees (2-3 inch caliper)
  - 100 native shrubs (appropriate for riparian zone)
  - Erosion control during planting phase
  - 3-year monitoring and maintenance plan with annual reports

Restoration plan details will be shown on Environmental Plan Sheet ENV-3.
