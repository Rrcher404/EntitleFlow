The Strategic Takeaway

Your platform shell is not just reusable scaffolding — it's a competitive moat. The auth-first architecture, model-agnostic AI layer, and enterprise RBAC would take a competitor 12+ weeks to rebuild from scratch. Every new vertical you launch on this shell ships in 2–3 weeks instead of 3–4 months.

The pattern that wins: "Upload document → AI extracts structured data → Teams collaborate on responses" is strongest in verticals where analysis is manual, incumbents are enterprise-only, and collaboration (not just extraction) is the value.

Recommended path: Keep building EntitleFlow (it's a strong vertical with real demand). When you're ready for product #2, ContractFlow (SMB legal) or ComplianceFlow (startup SOC 2/ISO) are the fastest to market — both reuse 90%+ of your shell and target segments where incumbents charge $50K+/year.
