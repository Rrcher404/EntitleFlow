Proposed Development: 210,000 sq ft Class A office, 850 spaces parking (surface)

===================================================================
PLANNING AND ZONING REVIEW
===================================================================

1. SETBACK COMPLIANCE - MAJOR
   Office building (Phase 2A) encroaches 6 feet into required 75-foot setback
   from Falls of Neuse Road. Relocate structure or submit setback variance with
   City Council. Must meet NCGS § 160D-602 variance standards (hardship,
   consistency with plan, no adverse effect on neighbors).

2. PARKING AREA BUFFER - MAJOR
   Surface parking on south side lacks screening buffer from adjacent residential
   (RC zoning). Provide 8-foot berm with opaque fencing and plant material per
   Raleigh Zoning Code § 27.2.3.1. Buffer must be established before occupancy.

3. TREE PRESERVATION - STANDARD
   Existing tree stand (42 trees, 12-24 inches DBH) is within Phase 2A footprint.
   Plan shows removal. Submit Tree Preservation Plan per RZC § 27.2.2.5 detailing
   protection measures and compensatory planting at 3:1 ratio (126 new trees minimum).

4. BUILDING HEIGHT - STANDARD
   Phase 2A building height shown as 65 feet. Confirm compliant with 75-foot maximum
   for this zoning district. Provide architectural elevation and measurement from
   highest grade point per RZC measurement standards.

5. PEDESTRIAN NETWORK - STANDARD
   No pedestrian walkway connection shown between Phase 1 (existing) and Phase 2
   parking areas. Provide continuous 8-foot sidewalk with accessible connections
   and crosswalk per ADA and NCDOT standards.

===================================================================
TRANSPORTATION AND TRAFFIC
===================================================================

6. DRIVEWAY LOCATION - MAJOR
   Main access driveway is only 210 feet from Falls of Neuse Road traffic signal.
   Minimum 400 feet required per NCGS § 136-66 and Raleigh Standard Plans. Relocate
   driveway or request spacing variance with traffic impact justification.

7. SIGHT DISTANCE - STANDARD
   Service drive sight triangle contains utility box. Remove obstruction or relocate
   utility. Must maintain 20-foot sight distance on each approach per AASHTO guidelines.

8. PARKING LOT DESIGN - STANDARD
   Interior drive aisles shown at 30 feet wide for two-way traffic. May reduce to 24
   feet per RZC § 27.2.4.2 for internal parking only. Saves 2.1 acres impervious area
   and reduces stormwater treatment costs.

9. LOADING AREA - MINOR
   Service dock area lacks trash enclosure. Provide screened enclosure per RZC § 27.2.5.4
   minimum 6 feet height, opaque screening on three sides, 12-foot wide opening minimum.

===================================================================
STORMWATER AND DRAINAGE
===================================================================

10. STORMWATER DETENTION - MAJOR
    Proposed bioretention basin is designed for 25-year storm event per NCAC T15A.02D.
    Submit calculations showing treatment of 1-inch storm (first flush, water quality)
    and routing of larger storms to detention. Currently plan shows detention only.

11. DETENTION BASIN OUTLET - STANDARD
    Outlet structure lacks sediment settling basin. Add forebay (minimum 8 feet x 12
    feet x 3 feet deep) upstream of outlet to trap sediment and reduce maintenance
    requirements. Provide cleanout access.

12. OUTFALL PROTECTION - STANDARD
    Stormwater outfall to unnamed tributary lacks velocity control and erosion
    protection. Provide 8-foot x 15-foot stabilized riprap apron per NC DWI BMP Manual
    with geotextile underdrain and vegetated banks downstream.

13. IMPERVIOUS AREA - MINOR
    Total impervious surface calculated at 76.4%. Provide on revised plan. If over
    75% of site, may trigger additional water quality requirements per stormwater
    variance procedures.

===================================================================
UTILITIES AND INFRASTRUCTURE
===================================================================

14. WATER SYSTEM IMPACT - MAJOR
    Water service to Phase 2 will be from 6-inch main on Falls of Neuse. Submit demand
    calculation (210,000 sq ft x 0.15 gpf per NCPC standard = 31,500 gpd). Coordinate
    with City of Raleigh Water Department for capacity analysis. May require
    transmission main upgrade.

15. SANITARY SEWER - STANDARD
    Phase 2 sanitary sewer connection length is 3,100 feet. Provide lift station design
    if gravity grade infeasible. Show all lift station components, electrical
    requirements, and utility coordination with existing Phase 1 sewer service.

16. UTILITY EASEMENTS - STANDARD
    Power and telecommunications easements shown on plans but not noted as "recorded"
    or "to be recorded." Clarify status and coordinate with utility providers for
    approval. Provide utility company letters of support or non-objection.

===================================================================
ARCHITECTURE AND BUILDING DESIGN
===================================================================

17. LOADING DOCK SCREENING - STANDARD
    Service/loading area is visible from Falls of Neuse Road. Provide architectural
    screening, earth berm, or combination. Minimum 6-foot height opaque screen per
    architectural guidelines.

18. ENTRY SIGNAGE - MINOR
    Monument sign shown (8 feet tall). Confirm compliance with Raleigh Zoning Code
    sign regulations § 27.8. Maximum 50 sq ft per sign, 8 feet height limit, one
    monument sign per roadway frontage.

===================================================================
NOTES AND NEXT STEPS

comments and demonstrating good-faith effort to resolve STANDARD comments. Minor
