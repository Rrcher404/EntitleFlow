CITY OF DURHAM
DEVELOPMENT REVIEW AND PERMITTING
SITE PLAN REVIEW COMMENTS

================================================================
PROJECT INFORMATION
================================================================
Project: Downtown Office Complex - Phase 1 & 2
Location: 318 Foster Street, Durham, NC 27701
Applicant: Catalyst Real Estate Partners
Site Plan #: 2026-SP-0341 (Phase 1), 2026-SP-0342 (Phase 2)
Review Date: March 11, 2026
Project Scope: 
  - Phase 1: 140,000 sq ft office tower (18 stories), 620 parking spaces (4 levels)
  - Phase 2 (Future): 90,000 sq ft office/retail, 350 spaces (surface & deck)
  Total Development: 18.7 acres (mixed-use downtown district)

================================================================
PLANNING & ZONING COMMENTS
================================================================

COMMENT 1 (CRITICAL)
Parking Requirement Conflict: Phase 1 shows 620 spaces, but code calculation (140,000
sq ft ÷ 250 sq ft per space) requires 560 spaces. Excess parking (60 spaces) may
require variance under Durham Code § 25-3.4.5.2. Either (a) reduce spaces to 560,
or (b) submit parking demand study justifying excess, or (c) apply for variance.
Excess parking increases stormwater treatment burden and walkability concerns.

COMMENT 2 (MAJOR)
Setback Variance - Building A: Tower setback from Foster Street is 35 feet; code
requires 50 feet for buildings over 100 feet in height (Durham Code § 25-4.2.1.3).
Submit variance application with design exception letter explaining mixed-use
compatibility and street activation justification.

COMMENT 3 (STANDARD)
Streetscape Improvements: Plan shows minimal street tree planting along Foster Street
frontage (only 4 trees for 280-foot frontage). Provide street trees at 30-foot
spacing minimum (9 trees), coordinate with City's streetscape improvement plan for
this corridor. Consider tree well design for underground parking structure constraints.

COMMENT 4 (STANDARD)
Ground-Floor Activation: Phase 1 site plan shows blank wall for 65 linear feet on
Foster Street (south face of parking deck). Provide retail, café, or active use
frontage per Durham downtown design guidelines. Window/transparency requirements:
minimum 60% of street-facing facade between 2-8 feet above grade.

COMMENT 5 (STANDARD)
Pedestrian Plaza: 0.8-acre plaza is shown but lacks design detail. Include plaza
amenities plan: seating, shade structures, public art location, sidewalk café
easement (if applicable), accessible routes, and stormwater infiltration features.
Durham prefers permeable paving in plazas (8.1 Permeable Pavement Manual).

================================================================
TRANSPORTATION COMMENTS
================================================================

COMMENT 6 (MAJOR)
Vehicular Access - Foster Street Driveway: Main parking deck entry is located 180
feet from Foster Street/Chapel Hill Road traffic signal. NCDOT spacing standard is
300 feet minimum from traffic signal per NCGS § 136-66. Coordinate with NCDOT for
potential signal timing adjustment or relocate driveway access.

COMMENT 7 (STANDARD)
Emergency Vehicle Access: Confirm secondary emergency access is available for parking
decks. Phase 1 parking structure shows only one stairwell/exit on Foster Street side.
Provide egress analysis and alternative evacuation route (interior connection to
Phase 2 area or secondary access point).

COMMENT 8 (STANDARD)
Truck Loading - Service Area: Loading dock faces Foster Street plaza area. Designate
dedicated truck loading zone with temporary closure capability during peak office
hours. Coordinate with City Transportation for restricted hours (e.g., 7-10 AM peak
traffic avoidance).

COMMENT 9 (MINOR)
Bicycle Parking: Plan shows 40 bike parking spaces (Section 25-3.4.4.1 requires 1
space per 7,500 sq ft, so 18-19 minimum required). Increase to 50 spaces and provide
covered/secure bicycle parking design details. Award LEED points for bicycle amenity.

================================================================
UTILITIES AND INFRASTRUCTURE
================================================================

COMMENT 10 (MAJOR)
Water Service Demand: Phase 1 demand is 210,000 GPD (estimated: 140,000 sq ft x 1.5
gpf = 210,000 gpd). Submit demand projection letter to Durham Water Department.
Coordinate with Water Department for 8-inch main extension along Chapel Hill Road.
May require water system improvement study under Durham Code § 25-6.2.

COMMENT 11 (STANDARD)
Sanitary Sewer Service: Phase 1 + Phase 2 combined demand is 425,000 GPD. Confirm
existing 12-inch sewer main on Chapel Hill Road can accept this capacity. Provide
capacity analysis from applicant's engineer. Durham utility service requires 10-day
advance notice for connection to critical infrastructure.

COMMENT 12 (STANDARD)
Stormwater Treatment Requirement: Total impervious area for Phase 1 is 12.3 acres.
Submit stormwater quality certification showing compliance with NCAC T15A.02D
(treatment of 1-inch event). Bioretention basin design appears adequate but provide
detailed calculations and sediment settling forebay design.

================================================================
ARCHITECTURE & URBAN DESIGN
================================================================

COMMENT 13 (STANDARD)
Building Height - Sun/Shadow Study: 18-story tower will create significant shadow
on adjacent residential neighborhood to north. Submit sun/shadow study (winter and
summer solstice) per Durham Design Guidelines Section 3.1.4. Assess impact on
historic neighborhoods and mitigation measures (setback adjustments, height
reduction, or design modifications).

COMMENT 14 (STANDARD)
Facade Materials: Specify high-quality facade materials appropriate for downtown
location. Discourage blank metal or concrete panels. Require transparency in ground-
floor commercial space. Coordinate with Downtown Master Plan design guidelines.

================================================================
ENVIRONMENTAL & SUSTAINABILITY COMMENTS
================================================================

COMMENT 15 (STANDARD)
Tree Preservation - Mature Street Trees: Four 60-year-old oaks exist along Foster
Street frontage (documented in survey). Protect during construction with tree
protection fencing per ANSI A300 standards. Prepare tree preservation plan by ISA
Certified Arborist. Compensation trees (2:1 ratio) required if any removals occur.

================================================================
NEXT STEPS
================================================================

Phase 1 (current): Address CRITICAL (Comment 1) and MAJOR comments (2, 6, 10) within
15 days. STANDARD comments within 25 days. Resubmittal packet should include revised
drawings and engineering studies addressing each point.

Phase 2 (future): Submit Phase 2 site plan addressing Phase 1 comments plus any
additional connectivity issues between phases.
