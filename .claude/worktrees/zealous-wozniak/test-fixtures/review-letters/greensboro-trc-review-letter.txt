CITY OF GREENSBORO
PLANNING DEPARTMENT
TECHNICAL REVIEW COMMITTEE (TRC) RESPONSE
================================================================================

Date: March 15, 2026
Project Name: Riverside Mixed-Use Development
Project Location: 1234 Riverside Drive, Greensboro, NC 27401
Applicant: Riverside Development Partners LLC
Special Use Permit #: SP-2026-0142
Parcel ID: 110532-45-07-66.00
Zoning: MU-2 (Mixed Use - Downtown Core)

This letter summarizes the results of the Technical Review Committee meeting held
on March 8, 2026. The following departments reviewed the site plan and associated
documents for the proposed mixed-use development with 85,000 sq ft retail/office,
120 residential units, and 450 parking spaces (mixed surface and structured).

================================================================================
TRANSPORTATION DEPARTMENT COMMENTS
================================================================================

COMMENT T-1 (MAJOR)
Driveway spacing: Primary retail entrance driveway is only 285 feet from Riverside
Drive intersection. Revise plan to locate at least 350 feet from intersection per
NCGS § 136-66 and City of Greensboro Development Standards Section 3.2.1. Include
spacing calculations on revised plan sheet.

COMMENT T-2 (MAJOR)
Sight triangle: The service entrance driveway does not maintain clear sight lines
per AASHTO standards. Remove or relocate landscaping and street furniture within
sight triangle (25 feet from driveway edge, 250 feet along Riverside Drive). Provide
sight distance diagram on revised plan.

COMMENT T-3 (STANDARD)
Left-turn lane: Provide dedicated left-turn lane for southbound Riverside Drive
traffic accessing retail entrance. Lane must be minimum 100 feet in length with
6-foot taper. Coordinate with NCDOT if any work within state right-of-way.

COMMENT T-4 (STANDARD)
Parking lot circulation: Reduce internal drive-aisle width from 28 feet to 24 feet
for interior drives not serving loading zones. This is per City standard and will
improve pedestrian connectivity and reduce impervious area.

COMMENT T-5 (STANDARD)
Pedestrian connectivity: Sidewalk connection from retail entrance to Riverside Drive
sidewalk is interrupted at parking lot edge. Provide continuous sidewalk with
cross-walk connection and accessible ramp per ADA standards.

================================================================================
STORMWATER MANAGEMENT DEPARTMENT COMMENTS
================================================================================

COMMENT S-1 (MAJOR)
BMP design: Proposed bioretention is undersized for 25-year storm (81.3 inches
annual rainfall per NCAC T15A.02D). Recalculate and provide calculations per
NC DWI Stormwater BMP Manual (2022 Update). Current sizing insufficient by 35%.

COMMENT S-2 (MAJOR)
Outfall stabilization: Outfall at northeast corner discharges to creek without
energy dissipation or stabilization. Provide rip-rap apron minimum 6 feet wide,
12 feet long per NC DWI specifications. Include erosion control details on
stormwater plan.

COMMENT S-3 (STANDARD)
Detention basin: Basin outlet structure lacks velocity control device. Add
multi-stage outlet orifice system to restrict discharge to pre-development rate
(0.08 cfs/acre for 2-year storm).

COMMENT S-4 (STANDARD)
Erosion control: Temporary construction BMPs (silt fence, inlet protection) are
shown but not detailed. Add construction sequencing and detail specifications
per NCAC T15A.02H. Specify maintenance frequency during construction.

COMMENT S-5 (MINOR)
Impervious area calculation: Sheet C-3 shows 78.2% but tabulation appears to
include proposed roofline. Recalculate excluding future building areas per NC DWI
methodology for undeveloped condition baseline.

================================================================================
ZONING AND PLANNING COMMENTS
================================================================================

COMMENT Z-1 (MAJOR)
Setback variance: Proposed building footprint encroaches 4 feet into required
50-foot riparian buffer. Although MU-2 zoning allows modification of riparian
buffer with Planning Director approval (NCGS § 160D-701), stream restoration must
be provided in compensatory mitigation area per Environmental Services comments
(see ENV-4).

COMMENT Z-2 (STANDARD)
Parking ratio: Proposed 450 spaces for 120 residential units (3.75 sp/unit) exceeds
City standard of 1.75 sp/unit for mixed-income housing. Reduce to 210 spaces or
justify need for additional spaces with lease/parking demand study.

COMMENT Z-3 (STANDARD)
Signage plan: Proposed monument sign is 12 feet tall; maximum permitted is 8 feet
for properties under 3 acres per Zoning Ordinance Section 8.3.4. Revise plan to
comply or submit variance application with design justification.

COMMENT Z-4 (MINOR)
Buffer planting: The 15-foot landscape buffer along Holden Road is shown with
deciduous trees only. Add 40% evergreen screening per Zoning Ordinance Section
7.2.3 to provide year-round visual buffer.

================================================================================
FIRE AND LIFE SAFETY DEPARTMENT COMMENTS
================================================================================

COMMENT F-1 (MAJOR)
Emergency access: Single access drive from Riverside Drive is insufficient. Provide
second emergency access from Holden Road per NFPA 101 Life Safety Code Section
7.5.1.2. Minimum 20-foot wide paved surface to all building exits.

COMMENT F-2 (STANDARD)
Hydrant spacing: Provide fire hydrants per NFPA 14 spacing requirements—one hydrant
per 5,000 sq ft of building area. Current plan shows 4 hydrants; calculations indicate
6 hydrants required. Revise water utility coordination.

COMMENT F-3 (STANDARD)
Sprinkler system: Proposed residential units require automatic sprinkler system per
NC Building Code § 903.2.1.1. Show system riser locations on architectural plans and
coordinate with utility routing on site plan.

================================================================================
LANDSCAPE ARCHITECTURE COMMENTS
================================================================================

COMMENT L-1 (MAJOR)
Tree save: Existing tree cluster in northeast corner (8 oaks, 65 DBH average) is not
identified for preservation. Revise plan to protect existing canopy or provide
compensatory mitigation planting at 2:1 ratio per Zoning Ordinance Section 7.1.2.
Submit ISA Certified Arborist assessment if removal is necessary.

COMMENT L-2 (STANDARD)
Landscape planting schedule: Street trees along Riverside Drive frontage are proposed
at 40-foot spacing; revise to 30-foot spacing per City streetscape standards. Species
selection of Zelkova serrata is appropriate for street tree (hardy to Zone 7b).

COMMENT L-3 (STANDARD)
Irrigation plan: Bioretention areas are to be irrigated with drip system for first
two growing seasons. Provide irrigation plan with valve locations, line sizing,
and maintenance schedule for establishment phase.

================================================================================
WATER AND SEWER UTILITY COMMENTS
================================================================================

COMMENT W-1 (MAJOR)
Water service: Proposed connection to 8-inch main on Riverside Drive will require
system impact study per Water Department policy. Submit demand projection (gallons/day)
for 120 units at single-family equivalent rates (300 gpd/unit). May require system
upgrade analysis.

COMMENT W-2 (STANDARD)
Sanitary sewer: Proposed connection point on Holden Road is 4,200 feet from site.
Provide lift station analysis showing minimum 5-foot depth and adequate slope for
gravity service. If gravity service infeasible, submit lift station design per
NCAC T15A.02U standards.

COMMENT W-3 (STANDARD)
Utility easements: Water main easement must be recorded 15 feet minimum from building
foundation per Water Department standards. Show easements on recorded plat and
coordinate with architectural site plan.

================================================================================
LANDSCAPE AND ENVIRONMENTAL SERVICES COMMENTS
================================================================================

COMMENT ENV-1 (MAJOR)
Stream assessment: Plan does not include Stream Quality Assessment (NCDWQ Form 1)
for creek impacting stormwater outfall. Submit Level 1 assessment by Environmental
consultant (state-certified) documenting existing stream conditions and proposed
impacts.

COMMENT ENV-2 (STANDARD)
Wetland delineation: No wetland delineation submitted. Site aerial shows potential
wetland area at northeast corner (darker vegetation patch). Submit Corps of Engineers
delineation or Environmental consultant assessment confirming no wetlands impact.

COMMENT ENV-3 (STANDARD)
Wildlife habitat: Site plan does not address wildlife corridors. Riparian buffer
restoration (ENV-4 mitigation) should prioritize native species supporting terrestrial
connectivity per NCAC T15A.02E guidance.

COMMENT ENV-4 (MAJOR)
Riparian restoration: To mitigate setback encroachment (Z-1), provide 0.25-acre
riparian buffer restoration upstream of development impact area. Plant 100 native
shrubs and 50 understory trees (2-3 inches caliper). Provide 3-year monitoring and
management plan.

================================================================================
GENERAL PLANNING AND CONDITIONS COMMENTS
================================================================================

COMMENT G-1 (STANDARD)
Phasing plan: Project proposes two phases (Phase 1: retail/office + surface parking,
Phase 2: residential + structured parking). Submit phasing timeline and interim site
stabilization measures. Phase 1 completion must not leave stormwater or traffic
impacts unresolved.

COMMENT G-2 (MINOR)
Conditions of approval: Proposed development approval is conditioned on:
(a) All TRC comments resolved to departments' satisfaction
(b) Riparian buffer restoration established and maintained (ENV-4)
(c) Secondary emergency access operational before occupancy (F-1)
(d) Parking ratios compliant or variance approved (Z-2)
(e) All utilities coordinated and approved in writing
