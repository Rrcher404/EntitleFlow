## Quick Start

### For social posting:
1. Open `posts/CAMPAIGN_POSTS.md` for ready-to-copy text content
2. Open any `.html` file in a browser for visual post assets
3. Screenshot or export at the rendered size for social uploads

### For NotebookLM studio:
1. Upload `notebooklm/NOTEBOOKLM_MASTER_PROMPT.md` as a source
2. Upload `brand/BRAND_VOICE.md` as a source
3. Add any relevant product/jurisdiction content as additional sources
4. Open `notebooklm/VIDEO_OVERVIEW_BRIEFS.md` and copy the prompt for your desired format
5. Paste into the "How would you like the video to be customized?" field
6. Generate

### For strategy reference:
- `brand/BRAND_VOICE.md` — the single source of truth for all content tone and terminology
- `strategy/MARKETING_STRATEGY.md` — campaign objectives, audience, channels, and budget
- `content-calendar/CONTENT_CALENDAR_Q2_2026.md` — what to publish, when, and where

## HTML Post Assets

All `.html` files in `posts/` are self-contained and render at social-media-ready sizes:
- **1200x628** — LinkedIn link preview / landscape posts
- **1080x1080** — Instagram / LinkedIn carousel slides
- Open in any browser, screenshot at native resolution, and upload to your social platform
- Colors and typography match the EntitleFlow V3 design system
