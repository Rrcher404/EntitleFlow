# EntitleFlow Marketing Strategy

**Version:** 1.0 | **Period:** Q2 2026 Platform Launch | **Owner:** Jene Maybury

---

## Executive Summary

EntitleFlow is launching as the first NC-specific land entitlement operations platform. Our go-to-market centers on founder-led outreach to architecture and civil firms in the Greensboro-Raleigh-Charlotte triangle, backed by content marketing that demonstrates workflow depth no generic platform can match. The strategy prioritizes trust, specificity, and operational credibility over volume.

---

## Campaign Objective (SMART)

**Generate 50 qualified walkthrough requests from NC-based architecture, civil, and development firms within 12 weeks of platform launch (Q2 2026), with a 30% conversion rate from walkthrough to paid Starter plan.**

Secondary objectives:
- Build an email list of 500+ NC land development professionals
- Establish EntitleFlow as the recognized NC permitting operations voice on LinkedIn
- Create a content library of 20+ assets for NotebookLM studio artifact generation

---

## Target Audience

### Primary ICP: The NC Firm PM
> "A project manager or associate at a 5-50 person architecture or civil engineering firm in North Carolina, running 3-10 active permit review cycles at any given time. They spend 10+ hours per permit cycle chasing reviewer comments across PDFs, emails, and portals. They want a system that gives them control without requiring an enterprise implementation."

**Demographics:** 28-45, mid-career, licensed architect or PE, based in the NC Piedmont Triad or Triangle
**Psychographics:** Pragmatic, skeptical of "another tool," values time savings over feature counts, trusts peer recommendations
**Channels:** LinkedIn, local AIA/ACEC chapters, industry email lists, NC-specific construction media
**Buying stage:** Problem-aware but solution-unaware — they know the chaos, they don't know there's a purpose-built tool

### Secondary ICP: The Builder-Developer
> "A development company principal or project coordinator managing 5-20 active projects across NC jurisdictions, relying on consultants for permit submissions but needing cleaner status visibility."

## Channel Strategy

### Tier 1: High-Priority Channels

#### LinkedIn (Organic + Targeted Ads)
- **Why:** Primary professional channel for NC AEC professionals
- **Cadence:** 4-5 posts/week (mix of thought leadership, product previews, jurisdiction insights)
- **Content types:** Carousel infographics, short-form text posts, video clips, workflow breakdowns
- **Paid:** Targeted ads to NC architects, civil engineers, PMs at 5-50 person firms
- **Budget:** $2,000/month

#### Email Marketing
- **Why:** Direct line to warm leads, walkthrough follow-ups, and nurture sequences
- **Cadence:** Weekly newsletter + triggered sequences (early access, post-walkthrough, onboarding)
- **Content:** Jurisdiction updates, workflow tips, product updates, case study teasers
- **Tool:** ConvertKit or Resend (founder-friendly, clean design)

#### Website (entitleflow.com)
- **Why:** Hub for all content, SEO, and conversion
- **Priority pages:** Homepage, Product, How It Works, Pricing, Jurisdiction guides (Greensboro, Raleigh)
- **SEO targets:** "NC permit tracking software", "reviewer comment management", "land entitlement workflow NC"

### Tier 2: Supporting Channels

#### NotebookLM Studio (Content Multiplier)
- **Why:** Generate slide decks, video overviews, quizzes, and infographics from structured source material
- **Usage:** Feed brand voice, product docs, and jurisdiction research into NotebookLM for artifact generation
- **Outputs:** Video overviews for social, slide decks for walkthroughs, quizzes for engagement

#### X/Twitter
- **Why:** Quick-hit thought leadership, industry conversations, product teasers
- **Cadence:** 3-4 posts/week, punchy and direct
- **Tone:** Operator voice — real talk about the chaos of NC permitting

#### YouTube / Video
- **Why:** Walkthrough demos, jurisdiction guides, product previews
- **Cadence:** 2 videos/month (can be generated from NotebookLM studio)
- **Types:** Platform overview, "Day in the life" workflow, jurisdiction deep dives

### Tier 3: Future / Experimental

- **Local AIA/ACEC chapter partnerships** (sponsorships, speaking, co-content)
- **NC construction trade media** (guest articles, press releases)
- **Podcast guest appearances** (AEC industry shows)
- **Webinars** (monthly, jurisdiction-specific topics)

## Content Pillars (Mapped to Calendar)

### Pillar 1: The Reviewer Comment Problem (40% of content)
Pain-point content that resonates with the daily frustration. Establishes that we understand the real work.
- "The 10 hours nobody budgets for"
- "Why your spreadsheet tracker breaks at cycle 3"
- "The comment-to-resubmittal gap that costs NC firms money"

### Pillar 2: NC Jurisdiction Intelligence (25% of content)
Specific, useful jurisdiction content that demonstrates depth and builds SEO authority.
- "Greensboro review cycle: what actually happens after submission"
- "Raleigh vs. Greensboro: where the workflows diverge"
- "Charlotte/Mecklenburg: what we know so far"

### Pillar 3: Product & Platform (20% of content)
Direct product content — features, previews, demos, comparisons.
- "Inside the reviewer comments workspace"
- "How EntitleFlow handles resubmittal coordination"
- "FlowE AI: what it does and what it doesn't"

### Pillar 4: Founder & Company Story (15% of content)
Personal, founder-led content that builds trust and humanizes the brand.
- "Why I started with North Carolina"
- "Building in public: weekly build updates"
- "What we learned from our first 10 walkthroughs"

## Campaign Phases

### Phase 1: Pre-Launch Warmup (Weeks 1-3)
- Publish brand voice and visual identity
- Begin LinkedIn posting cadence (3x/week)
- Launch early access landing page with email capture
- Seed NotebookLM with all source material
- Create first 5 social post assets

### Phase 2: Platform Launch (Weeks 4-6)
- Launch announcement post (LinkedIn, X, email)
- Product walkthrough video (NotebookLM studio)
- Jurisdiction guide drops (Greensboro, Raleigh)
- Begin paid LinkedIn ads targeting NC AEC professionals
- Activate email nurture sequence for early access list

### Phase 3: Momentum & Proof (Weeks 7-12)
- Weekly content cadence (4-5 LinkedIn, 3-4 X, 1 email)
- First case study / testimonial from pilot users
- Begin webinar series (monthly, jurisdiction topics)
- Ramp paid ads based on performance data
- Publish comparison content (EntitleFlow vs. manual vs. generic)
