**Purpose:** Ready-to-paste customization prompts for NotebookLM's "Customize Video Overview" feature. Each brief is optimized for a specific format (Cinematic, Explainer, Brief) and use case. Copy the prompt text directly into the "How would you like the video to be customized?" field.

## How to Use This Document

1. Open your NotebookLM notebook (should contain the Master Source Prompt + Brand Voice + relevant content sources)
2. Click "Generate" on the Audio/Video Overview
3. Select the format (Cinematic, Explainer, or Brief)
4. Paste the appropriate prompt below into the customization field
5. Optionally add a quick-tag: "+ Strategic Overview", "+ Technical Deep Dive", or "+ Actionable Setup Guide"
6. Click Generate

## TIPS FOR BEST RESULTS

1. **Always have the Master Source Prompt loaded** as a source in the notebook before generating
2. **Add specific content sources** alongside the master prompt for more targeted videos (e.g., add a Greensboro jurisdiction guide for jurisdiction-specific content)
3. **Use the Cinematic format** for top-of-funnel awareness content (LinkedIn, website)
4. **Use the Explainer format** for mid-funnel education content (YouTube, webinars, sales)
5. **Use the Brief format** for bottom-funnel conversion content (email, social clips, quick shares)
6. **Regenerate freely** — each generation will be slightly different; pick the best take
7. **Export and trim** — NotebookLM videos can be trimmed in any video editor for social clips
