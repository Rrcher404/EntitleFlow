## CONTEXT: What is EntitleFlow?

EntitleFlow is a North Carolina-first land entitlement operations platform — the operating system for teams managing development approval workflows. It solves a specific, painful problem: after a permit set is submitted to a municipality, teams receive PDF redline packages full of reviewer comments with no structured way to track, assign, respond to, or resolve them. EntitleFlow replaces spreadsheets, email chains, and ad-hoc trackers with a structured workspace for the post-submission review cycle.

## THE PROBLEM (Use this for pain-point framing)

After a permit application is submitted to an NC jurisdiction (Greensboro, Raleigh, Charlotte, etc.), the real chaos begins:

1. **Reviewer comments arrive as unstructured PDFs** — redlines, markups, and email attachments scattered across reviewers from planning, fire, engineering, utilities, and other departments
2. **No single system tracks comments** — teams copy comments into spreadsheets, email threads, or personal notes. Different team members have different versions.
3. **Ownership is unclear** — who is responsible for responding to which comment? Which discipline lead owns which issue?
4. **Resubmittals are reactive** — teams scramble before deadlines to figure out what changed, what is still open, and what needs to travel in the next package
5. **Status is invisible** — principals, PMs, and clients ask "where are we?" and the answer requires a manual reconstruction of the current state
6. **Institutional knowledge walks out the door** — when a PM leaves, their process notes, jurisdiction knowledge, and reviewer relationships leave with them

**The cost:** 10+ hours of coordinator/PM time per review cycle, missed comments that trigger additional cycles, strained client relationships from lack of visibility, and zero compounding knowledge across projects.

---

## THE SOLUTION (Use this for product framing)

### Four Core Modules

**1. Reviewer Comment Management**
Turn unstructured reviewer comments into structured issues with owners, statuses, response language, and cycle tracking. Stop losing comments in email threads and PDF markups.

**2. Resubmittal Coordination**
See what changed, what is still open, and what must travel in the next submission package. Make the resubmittal process proactive instead of reactive.

**3. NC Jurisdiction Intelligence**
Search jurisdiction workflows, departments, portals, submission touchpoints, and known process friction for Greensboro, Raleigh, Charlotte, and other NC jurisdictions.

**4. Approval Workflow Visibility**
Give principals, PMs, and clients one shared operational view of where approvals stand — tied to real events and next steps, not a recreated status email.

### AI Layer: FlowE Agents
- **Document Parser:** AI-powered extraction of reviewer comments from uploaded PDFs
- **Comment Classifier:** Automatic categorization by discipline, severity, and response type
- **Response Suggester:** Draft response language based on comment type and jurisdiction context
- **Compliance Checker:** Flag potential code and regulation alignment issues
- **Workflow Advisor:** Proactive guidance on process steps and deadlines
- **Knowledge Synthesizer:** Cross-project learning and pattern recognition
